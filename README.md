# KyeDAO: Decentralized Rotating Savings & Credit Association

KyeDAO is a decentralized protocol that modernizes the traditional Korean "Kye" system - a rotating savings and credit association (ROSCA) commonly used in immigrant communities. By leveraging blockchain technology and DAO governance, KyeDAO aims to make community-based lending more secure, transparent, and accessible.

## Background

Kye systems have historically served as an important financial tool for Korean immigrant communities, allowing members to pool money together and take turns receiving the accumulated funds. However, traditional Kye systems face several challenges:

- Rely on a single trusted party to manage all pooled funds
- Lack of transparency in fund management
- Risk of mismanagement or fraud
- Limited scalability beyond close-knit communities

## Solution

KyeDAO addresses these challenges by:

- Implementing smart contracts to automate and secure fund management
- Using DAO governance for democratic decision-making
- Providing full transparency of all transactions on-chain
- Eliminating single points of failure through decentralization

## Core Features

- **Secure Asset Pooling**: Members can safely deposit and withdraw funds
- **Automated Round Management**: Smart contracts handle contribution cycles and distributions
- **DAO Governance**: Community members can create and vote on proposals
- **Transparent Operations**: All transactions and decisions are recorded on-chain
- **Member Management**: Controlled addition and removal of participants

## Technical Implementation

The protocol consists of two main smart contract versions:

1. **KyeDaoV1**: Implements basic rotating round functionality
2. **KyeDaoV2**: Adds proposal-based bidding model for enhanced flexibility
